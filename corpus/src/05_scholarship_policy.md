# Scholarship and Fee Concession Policy

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: SF | Office of the Dean (Student Welfare) | Approved by the Board of Governors, 3 May 2026

## 1. Objective

### 1.1 Purpose
This Policy sets out the scholarships and fee concessions offered by the Institute to students of the B.Tech. programme, the conditions of eligibility and continuation, and the procedure for application and disbursement. It is to be read together with the Academic Regulations and the Fee Schedule and Refund Rules.

### 1.2 Funding
Scholarships under this Policy are funded from the Institute's own resources and from endowments received from donors. The total number of scholarships in a year is decided by the Board of Governors on the recommendation of the Scholarship Committee.

## 2. Categories of Scholarship

### 2.1 Merit Scholarship
The Merit Scholarship is awarded to students who rank in the top five per cent of their branch by CGPA at the end of an academic year. The scholarship is a waiver of fifty per cent of the tuition fee for the following academic year. In the first year, the scholarship is awarded on the basis of the rank in the entrance examination through which the student was admitted.

### 2.2 Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
The Merit-cum-Means Scholarship is awarded to students whose annual family income from all sources does not exceed Rs. 3,00,000 and who have a CGPA of not less than 7.0 at the end of the preceding academic year or, for first-year students, not less than 80 per cent marks in the qualifying examination. The scholarship is a waiver of twenty-five per cent of the tuition fee. An income certificate issued by a Tehsildar or an officer of equivalent rank, not older than six months, must accompany the application.

### 2.3 Sports Scholarship
The Sports Scholarship is awarded to students who have represented the State or the country in a sport recognised by the Association of Indian Universities, and is a waiver of twenty-five per cent of the tuition fee. Recipients are required to represent the Institute in the relevant sport whenever called upon by the Dean (Student Welfare).

### 2.4 Restriction on Multiple Scholarships
A student may hold only one scholarship under this Policy at a time. A student eligible for more than one scholarship shall be awarded the scholarship of the higher value.

## 3. Conditions of Continuation

### 3.1 Academic Performance
A Merit Scholarship continues for the following academic year only if the student maintains a CGPA of not less than 8.0 at the end of each semester. A student whose CGPA falls below 8.0 in any semester forfeits the scholarship for the remaining period of that academic year. The Merit-cum-Means Scholarship continues only if the student maintains a CGPA of not less than 7.0 at the end of each semester.

### 3.2 Conduct
A scholarship is withdrawn with immediate effect if the student is found guilty of ragging, of the use of unfair means in an examination, or of any other act for which a penalty is imposed by the Institute Disciplinary Committee.

### 3.3 Attendance
A scholarship holder who is detained in any course for shortage of attendance forfeits the scholarship for the remaining period of that academic year.

## 4. Application Procedure

### 4.1 Application Window
Applications for all scholarships must be submitted on the scholarship portal within thirty days of the commencement of the odd semester. Applications received after the window closes are not considered for that academic year.

### 4.2 Documents
An application must be accompanied by the grade card of the preceding year, the income certificate where applicable, sports certificates where applicable, and a declaration that the student does not hold any other scholarship of the Institute.

### 4.3 Decision
The Scholarship Committee, consisting of the Dean (Student Welfare) as chair, the Finance Officer, and two faculty members nominated by the Vice-Chancellor, shall decide on the applications within forty-five days of the closing of the application window and shall publish the list of recipients on the student portal.

## 5. Disbursement

### 5.1 Form of Disbursement
All scholarships under this Policy are disbursed as a waiver of the tuition fee, adjusted against the fee payable for the even semester of the academic year of award and the odd semester of the following academic year. No amount is paid in cash or credited to the student's bank account.

### 5.2 Scholarship Awarded after Payment of Fee
Where a student has already paid the full tuition fee before the award of a scholarship, the amount of the waiver shall be adjusted against the fee payable for the next semester.

## 6. Fee Concessions and Refunds

### 6.1 Wards of Institute Staff
Children of permanent employees of the Institute are entitled to a concession of fifty per cent of the tuition fee for the duration of the programme, subject to satisfactory academic progress as certified by the Dean.

### 6.2 Refund on Withdrawal before Commencement of Classes
A student who withdraws admission before the commencement of classes shall be refunded the tuition fee paid, after the deduction of a processing charge of ten per cent of the tuition fee. The caution deposit shall be refunded in full.

### 6.3 Recovery on Withdrawal
A scholarship holder who withdraws from the programme during an academic year shall repay the amount of the waiver received for the semester in which the withdrawal takes place before a no-dues certificate is issued.

## 7. Grievances

### 7.1 Grievance Redressal
A student aggrieved by a decision of the Scholarship Committee may submit a written grievance to the Dean (Student Welfare) within fifteen days of the publication of the list of recipients. The Dean shall dispose of the grievance within thirty days. No further appeal lies against the decision of the Dean, except to the Vice-Chancellor on a question of interpretation of this Policy.
