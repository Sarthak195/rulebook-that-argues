# Examination and Evaluation Regulations

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: EE | Issued by the Controller of Examinations | Effective 1 July 2026

## 1. Conduct of End-Semester Examinations

### 1.1 Schedule
The timetable of the end-semester examinations shall be published on the examination portal at least fifteen days before the commencement of the examinations. Requests for a change of date shall not be entertained from individual students. A clash between two courses registered by a student must be reported to the Controller of Examinations within three working days of the publication of the timetable.

### 1.2 Hall Ticket
No student shall be admitted to the examination hall without a valid hall ticket issued by the Controller of Examinations and the Institute identity card. Hall tickets are issued to students who have satisfied the attendance requirement, have no outstanding tuition fee dues, and have registered for the course.

### 1.3 Reporting and Entry
Students shall report to the examination hall thirty minutes before the scheduled start of the examination. No student shall be admitted to the hall after thirty minutes from the scheduled start. No student shall leave the hall within the first sixty minutes of the examination or during the last fifteen minutes.

### 1.4 Seating
Seating arrangements are displayed outside the examination hall one hour before the examination. A student found occupying a seat other than the one allotted shall be reported to the Chief Superintendent.

## 2. Materials in the Examination Hall

### 2.1 Permitted Materials
A student may bring into the examination hall the hall ticket, the Institute identity card, blue or black ball-point pens, pencils, an eraser, a transparent water bottle, and any other item expressly permitted on the question paper.

### 2.2 Prohibited Materials
Mobile phones, smart watches, wireless earphones and any other communication device, as well as books, notes and printed or handwritten material of any kind, are prohibited in the examination hall. Possession of a prohibited item, whether or not it is used, is treated as the use of unfair means under Section 4 of these Regulations. Bags must be deposited at the location indicated by the Chief Superintendent.

## 3. Absence from Examination

### 3.1 Absence on Medical Grounds
A student who is unable to appear for an end-semester examination because of illness shall submit a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner and countersigned by the Institute Medical Officer to the Controller of Examinations within five working days of the date of the examination. On acceptance of the certificate, the student shall be permitted to appear for the course in the next supplementary examination, and that appearance shall be treated as the first attempt and shall be exempt from the supplementary examination fee.

### 3.2 Absence on Institute Deputation
A student who is deputed by the Dean (Student Welfare) to represent the Institute during the period of the end-semester examinations shall be permitted to appear for the affected courses in the next supplementary examination as a first attempt, on production of the deputation letter, without payment of the supplementary examination fee.

### 3.3 Missed Mid-term Tests
A student who misses a mid-term test on medical grounds and submits a medical certificate to the HoD within five working days of the test shall be given a re-test within two weeks of rejoining classes. The re-test is of the same standard and duration as the original test and is set by the course instructor.

## 4. Unfair Means

### 4.1 Definition
The following constitute the use of unfair means: possession of any prohibited material under Section 2.2; copying from, or permitting copying by, another student; communicating with any person other than the invigilator; writing on any material other than the answer book; exchanging answer books or question papers; appealing to the examiner in the answer book; and any other act which, in the opinion of the Unfair Means Committee, is intended to obtain an undue advantage in the examination.

### 4.2 Penalties
For a first offence, the examination in the course concerned shall be cancelled and the student shall be awarded the grade F in that course. For a second offence, in the same or any subsequent examination, all examinations of the semester shall be cancelled and the student shall be awarded the grade F in all courses of that semester. A third offence shall result in expulsion from the Institute. Possession of a mobile phone in the examination hall shall be treated as a first offence even if the phone is switched off.

### 4.3 Unfair Means Committee
Every reported case shall be placed before the Unfair Means Committee, consisting of the Controller of Examinations, two faculty members nominated by the Vice-Chancellor, and the HoD of the student's department. The student shall be given an opportunity to be heard. The Committee shall record its decision within fifteen days of the incident.

### 4.4 Appeal
A student aggrieved by a decision of the Unfair Means Committee may appeal in writing to the Vice-Chancellor within seven days of the communication of the decision. The decision of the Vice-Chancellor on the appeal shall be final.

## 5. Evaluation of Answer Scripts

### 5.1 Coding and Evaluation
Answer scripts are coded to conceal the identity of the student before evaluation. Each script is evaluated by an examiner appointed by the Controller of Examinations from a panel approved by the Board of Studies.

### 5.2 Moderation
Where the failure rate in a course exceeds forty per cent, the scripts shall be moderated by a second examiner, and the Board of Examiners may recommend a uniform adjustment of marks in that course. Moderation is applied to all students of the course uniformly and is not available on individual request.

## 6. Declaration of Results

### 6.1 Timeline
The results of the end-semester examinations shall be declared on the examination portal within thirty days of the last examination of the semester.

### 6.2 Provisional Results
Results are provisional until the grade card is issued and are subject to the correction of any clerical error detected by the Controller of Examinations within sixty days of the declaration.

### 6.3 Re-evaluation
Applications for re-evaluation and re-totalling are governed by Section 9 of the Academic Regulations.

## 7. Supplementary Examination Fee

### 7.1 Fee
The fee for a supplementary examination is Rs. 800 per course, payable within seven days of the notification of the supplementary examination on the examination portal. The fee is payable through the online payment portal only.

### 7.2 Exemption
Students permitted to appear as a first attempt under Section 3.1 or Section 3.2 are exempt from the supplementary examination fee for that appearance.

## 8. Practical Examinations

### 8.1 Conduct
End-semester practical examinations are conducted by an internal examiner and an external examiner from another institution. The external examiner is appointed by the Controller of Examinations from a panel approved by the Board of Studies, and the marks awarded jointly by the two examiners are final.

### 8.2 Laboratory Record
A student who does not submit the completed laboratory record, certified by the course instructor, shall not be admitted to the end-semester practical examination.
