# Hostel Handbook

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: HH | Office of the Chief Warden | Edition 2026-27

## 1. Allotment of Rooms

### 1.1 Eligibility and Priority
Hostel accommodation is available to full-time students of the Institute and is allotted for one academic year at a time. Priority is given to students whose permanent residence is more than forty kilometres from the Institute, followed by students with a higher CGPA in the preceding year. First-year students are allotted rooms in the order of their admission rank.

### 1.2 Application
Applications for hostel accommodation must be submitted on the hostel portal within seven days of the publication of the academic calendar. Late applications are considered only against vacancies. Allotment letters are issued by the Chief Warden, and the room must be occupied within five days of the date of the letter, failing which the allotment lapses.

### 1.3 Change of Room
A resident may apply to the Warden for a change of room after the first month of the semester. Changes are permitted only with the written approval of the Warden and only once in an academic year. An unauthorised exchange of rooms attracts a fine of Rs. 1,000 for each resident involved.

## 2. Timings and Movement

### 2.1 Hostel Gate Timings
The hostel gates close at 10:30 p.m. every day. Residents returning after the gates close must sign the late-entry register maintained by the security staff and state the reason for the delay. The gates open at 5:30 a.m.

### 2.2 Late Entries
A resident who records three late entries in a calendar month shall be issued a written warning by the Warden, and a copy shall be sent to the parent or guardian. A resident who records five late entries in a calendar month shall be fined Rs. 500 and may be asked to vacate the hostel on repetition.

### 2.3 Night Out and Leave
A resident who wishes to stay outside the hostel overnight must obtain the prior written approval of the Warden at least twenty-four hours in advance, supported by the consent of the parent or guardian recorded on the hostel portal. Residents leaving for vacations or for more than two nights must record the departure and return dates in the leave register at the hostel office.

### 2.4 Roll Call
Roll call is taken in every hostel at 10:45 p.m. by the hostel supervisor. A resident absent at roll call without approved leave is treated as a late entry under Section 2.2.

## 3. Visitors

### 3.1 Visiting Hours
Visitors are permitted only in the visitors' lounge on the ground floor of each hostel, between 4:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. on all days. Every visitor must enter their name, the name of the resident visited, and the time of entry and exit in the visitors' register.

### 3.2 Visitors in Rooms
No visitor is permitted on the residential floors or in the rooms of the hostel. Parents or guardians may be permitted to visit a resident's room with the prior permission of the Warden, on the day of admission to or vacating of the hostel, or in the case of the resident's illness.

### 3.3 Residents of Other Hostels
A resident of one hostel is not permitted to enter another hostel after 9:00 p.m. or to stay overnight in another hostel at any time.

## 4. Mess

### 4.1 Compulsory Mess
Every resident must be a member of the hostel mess. Mess charges are payable in advance for the semester along with the hostel fee, on the dates given in the Fee Schedule.

### 4.2 Mess Timings
Breakfast is served from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., lunch from 12:30 p.m. to 2:00 p.m., evening tea from 5:00 p.m. to 5:45 p.m., and dinner from 7:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Meals are not served outside these hours, and food is not to be carried to the rooms except for a resident who is unwell, with the permission of the mess supervisor.

### 4.3 Mess Rebate
A resident who is absent from the hostel for a continuous period of seven days or more is entitled to a rebate of mess charges for the period of absence, provided that the absence is recorded in the leave register in advance and the rebate form is submitted within seven days of return. No rebate is admissible for an absence of less than seven days.

### 4.4 Mess Committee
Each hostel elects a mess committee of five residents every semester. The mess committee finalises the weekly menu in consultation with the mess contractor and reports complaints about the quality of food to the Warden.

## 5. Prohibited Activities and Items

### 5.1 Alcohol, Tobacco and Drugs
Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco products or narcotic substances within the hostel premises, or entering the hostel in an intoxicated state, shall result in immediate expulsion from the hostel and referral to the Institute Disciplinary Committee.

### 5.2 Electrical Appliances
The use of electric heaters, immersion rods, induction cooktops, electric kettles, irons and any appliance rated above 500 watts in hostel rooms is prohibited. Such appliances shall be confiscated and a fine of Rs. 1,000 shall be imposed for each occurrence. Residents may use laptops, mobile chargers, table lamps and table fans.

### 5.3 Ragging
Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited within and outside the hostel premises, in accordance with the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009. Any incident of ragging shall be reported to the Anti-Ragging Committee, and a student found guilty of ragging shall be expelled from the hostel and shall be liable to the punishments prescribed under the said Regulations, including expulsion from the Institute.

### 5.4 Other Prohibitions
Gambling, playing loud music, the use of firecrackers, and the display of posters or writing on walls are prohibited. Residents are not permitted to keep guests, including relatives, overnight in their rooms.

## 6. Discipline and Fines

### 6.1 Damage to Property
A resident who damages hostel property shall pay the cost of repair or replacement as assessed by the Estate Office, together with a penalty of Rs. 500. Where the person responsible for damage in a common area cannot be identified, the cost shall be recovered equally from all residents of the floor or wing concerned.

### 6.2 Silence Hours
Silence must be maintained in the hostels between 11:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. Playing music audible outside the room, shouting, or gathering in corridors during silence hours attracts a fine of Rs. 200 for a first occurrence and Rs. 500 thereafter.

### 6.3 Cleanliness
Residents are responsible for keeping their rooms clean. Housekeeping staff clean the rooms twice a week on the days notified by the Warden, and residents must allow access to their rooms at those times. Rooms are inspected by the Warden once a month.

## 7. Vacating the Hostel

### 7.1 End of Semester
Residents must vacate their rooms within three days of their last end-semester examination unless permitted in writing by the Warden to stay for a project, an internship or a supplementary examination. Belongings left in a room after this period shall be removed and stored at the resident's risk for thirty days, after which they shall be disposed of.

### 7.2 Refund of Hostel Caution Deposit
The hostel caution deposit shall be refunded within sixty days of the resident vacating the hostel, after the deduction of any fines, damages or mess dues outstanding, provided that the resident has submitted the room-vacating form countersigned by the hostel supervisor.

## 8. Health and Emergencies

### 8.1 Illness
A resident who falls ill must inform the hostel supervisor or the Warden immediately. The Institute Health Centre is open from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., and a doctor on call and an ambulance are available at all hours through the hostel office.

### 8.2 Emergency Contacts
The emergency contact numbers of the Warden, the Chief Warden, the Health Centre and the security office are displayed on the notice board of every floor. Residents must keep their own emergency contact details on the hostel portal up to date.
