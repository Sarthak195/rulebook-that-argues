# Student Activities, Discipline and Grievance Policy

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: SG | Office of the Dean (Student Welfare) | Notified 1 July 2026

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Applicability
This Policy governs the Student Council, registered student clubs, the conduct of student events, the disciplinary procedure for students, and the redressal of student grievances. It applies to all students of the Institute. Matters of examination malpractice are governed by the Examination and Evaluation Regulations and are not covered by this Policy.

## 2. Student Council

### 2.1 Composition
The Student Council consists of a President, a Vice-President, a General Secretary, a Treasurer, and one representative from each branch and each year of study. Office bearers are elected for one academic year and may not hold the same office for more than two terms.

### 2.2 Eligibility to Contest
A student may contest an election to the Student Council only if the student has a CGPA of not less than 6.5, has no F or DT grade in the preceding semester, is not on academic probation, and has not been subjected to any disciplinary action during the programme.

### 2.3 Elections
Elections are held in the fourth week of the odd semester under the supervision of the Dean (Student Welfare). Campaigning is permitted for seven days before the election. Printed posters are permitted only on the notice boards designated for the purpose, and a candidate's total campaign expenditure shall not exceed Rs. 2,000.

## 3. Student Clubs

### 3.1 Registration
A group of at least fifteen students may apply to register a club by submitting a constitution, a list of members and the name of a faculty advisor to the Dean (Student Welfare). Registration is valid for one academic year and must be renewed by submitting an annual report and audited accounts.

### 3.2 Funding
A registered club may receive an annual grant of up to Rs. 25,000 from the Student Activities Fund on the recommendation of its faculty advisor. Grants are released in two instalments, and the second instalment is released only after the utilisation certificate for the first instalment has been submitted to the Finance Office.

### 3.3 Permission for Events
An event organised by a club or by the Student Council requires the written permission of the Dean (Student Welfare), applied for at least ten working days before the event, stating the venue, the expected attendance, the budget and the names of the faculty members who will be present. Events after 9:00 p.m. require the additional permission of the Chief Warden.

### 3.4 External Participation
A student who represents the Institute in an inter-collegiate event must carry a deputation letter issued by the Dean (Student Welfare), and the attendance provisions of the Leave and Medical Policy apply to the period of deputation.

## 4. Disciplinary Procedure

### 4.1 Institute Disciplinary Committee
The Institute Disciplinary Committee consists of the Dean (Student Welfare) as chair, two faculty members nominated by the Vice-Chancellor, the Chief Warden, and one student member nominated by the Student Council. The Committee considers all cases of misconduct referred to it by a Head of Department, a Warden or the Dean (Academics).

### 4.2 Show-cause Notice
No penalty shall be imposed on a student without a written show-cause notice stating the alleged misconduct and giving the student not less than five working days to reply in writing. The student may appear before the Committee in person and may be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

### 4.3 Penalties
The Committee may impose a written warning, a fine of up to Rs. 10,000, suspension from classes for up to one semester, expulsion from the hostel, or expulsion from the Institute, having regard to the gravity of the misconduct and any previous record. A penalty of suspension or expulsion shall be reported to the Vice-Chancellor within three working days.

### 4.4 Appeal
A student may appeal against a decision of the Institute Disciplinary Committee to the Vice-Chancellor in writing within seven days of the communication of the decision. The Vice-Chancellor shall dispose of the appeal within thirty days, and the decision of the Vice-Chancellor shall be final.

## 5. Grievance Redressal

### 5.1 Grievance Redressal Cell
The Institute maintains a Grievance Redressal Cell consisting of the Dean (Academics) as chair, one faculty member from each school, and a student member nominated by the Student Council. A student may submit a grievance relating to academic, administrative or hostel matters through the grievance portal or in writing to the Cell.

### 5.2 Timelines
The Cell shall acknowledge a grievance within three working days of its receipt and shall communicate its decision within fifteen working days. Where a grievance requires the response of a department, the department shall respond to the Cell within seven working days of being asked.

### 5.3 Escalation
A student who is not satisfied with the decision of the Grievance Redressal Cell may escalate the grievance to the Vice-Chancellor within ten days of the communication of the decision. Grievances relating to sexual harassment are referred to the Internal Complaints Committee constituted under the applicable law and are not dealt with by the Cell.

### 5.4 Protection against Victimisation
No student shall be subjected to any adverse action for having submitted a grievance in good faith. A finding that a grievance was malicious or frivolous may itself be referred to the Institute Disciplinary Committee.

## 6. Use of Institute Facilities

### 6.1 Auditorium and Seminar Halls
The auditorium and the seminar halls may be booked by a registered club or by the Student Council through the Estate Office at least five working days in advance. Bookings are free of charge for academic and cultural events of the Institute; a refundable security deposit of Rs. 5,000 is payable for events with external participants.

### 6.2 Sports Facilities
The sports complex is open to all students from 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Equipment may be borrowed against the Institute identity card and must be returned the same day. Damage to equipment is charged at the replacement cost.
