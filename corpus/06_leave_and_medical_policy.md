# Leave and Medical Policy for Students

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: LM | Office of the Dean (Academics) | Notified 22 June 2026

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Applicability
This Policy governs leave of absence from classes, the treatment of absence on medical grounds, and the procedure for obtaining and submitting medical certificates. It applies to all students of the Institute, whether resident in the hostels or otherwise.

### 1.2 Relationship with Other Regulations
This Policy supplements the attendance provisions of the Academic Regulations and the provisions of the Examination and Evaluation Regulations relating to absence from examinations. Nothing in this Policy shall be construed as relaxing the requirement of registration for a semester.

## 2. Medical Leave

### 2.1 Medical Certificate
A student who is absent from classes on account of illness must obtain a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner stating the nature of the illness and the period of rest advised. The certificate must be countersigned by the Institute Medical Officer and submitted to the HoD within seven days of the student rejoining classes. Certificates submitted after this period are not accepted, and the absence is treated as unauthorised.

### 2.2 Condonation of Attendance Shortfall on Medical Grounds
Where a student's attendance in a course falls short of the minimum required on account of certified illness, the shortfall may be condoned by the Dean on the recommendation of the HoD, provided that the student's overall attendance in the course is not less than 55 per cent. The Dean may call for the original medical records before granting condonation, and condonation is recorded on the academic portal against the course concerned.

### 2.3 Hospitalisation
A student hospitalised for more than seven days must inform the HoD and the Dean through the Institute Medical Officer within three days of admission to the hospital, through a parent, guardian or fellow student where the student is unable to do so. The Institute Medical Officer shall visit or contact the student and advise the Dean on the likely period of absence.

### 2.4 Prolonged Illness
A student whose illness is likely to result in absence from more than half of the instructional days of a semester shall be advised to apply for a break of study under Section 10 of the Academic Regulations, and the Institute Medical Officer shall certify the need for the break.

## 3. Other Leave

### 3.1 Leave for Institute Deputation
A student deputed in writing by the Dean (Student Welfare) to represent the Institute in a competition, a conference, a cultural event, or an NCC or NSS camp shall be marked present for the period of deputation, provided that the deputation letter is submitted to the HoD within three working days of the student's return.

### 3.2 Bereavement Leave
In the event of the death of a parent, sibling, grandparent or spouse, a student shall be granted bereavement leave of up to seven days, which shall be counted as attendance. The student shall inform the HoD by e-mail or through the student portal before or during the leave, and shall submit a written application on return.

### 3.3 Leave for Placement Interviews
A student attending a placement interview or test arranged through the Institute's Training and Placement Cell shall be marked present for the duration of the interview or test, on production of the call letter countersigned by the Placement Officer.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Leave Application
All leave, other than bereavement leave, must be applied for in advance on the student portal, stating the reason and the period of leave, and must be approved by the HoD. Leave applied for after the period of absence is not approved, except in the case of illness supported by a medical certificate under Section 2.1.

### 4.2 Leave Register
The Department maintains a leave register recording all leave approved under this Policy. Attendance condoned or counted as present under this Policy is entered on the academic portal by the HoD, and the student must verify the entry within three working days.

### 4.3 False Certificates
A student who submits a false or altered medical certificate or deputation letter shall be referred to the Institute Disciplinary Committee, and the absence shall be treated as unauthorised. The Disciplinary Committee may impose a penalty up to and including expulsion from the Institute.

## 5. Health Services

### 5.1 Institute Health Centre
The Institute Health Centre provides free consultation to all students on working days between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. Medicines prescribed by the Institute Medical Officer are provided free of charge for a period of up to three days; longer courses must be purchased by the student.

### 5.2 Medical Insurance
Every student is covered under the group medical insurance scheme of the Institute for hospitalisation expenses of up to Rs. 1,00,000 per year. The premium is included in the development fee. Claims are made through the Institute Medical Officer within thirty days of discharge from the hospital.
