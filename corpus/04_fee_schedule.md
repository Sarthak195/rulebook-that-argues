# Fee Schedule and Refund Rules (2026-27)

Shivalik Institute of Technology, Indore
Document code: FS | Finance Office | Applicable to all B.Tech. students

## 1. Fee Components

### 1.1 Schedule of Fees
The following fees are payable by every B.Tech. student. All amounts are in Indian Rupees.

| Fee component | Amount (Rs.) | Frequency | Payable by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuition fee | 85,000 | Per semester | All students |
| Examination fee | 2,500 | Per semester | All students |
| Development and laboratory fee | 6,000 | Per semester | All students |
| Hostel room rent | 45,000 | Per semester | Hostel residents |
| Mess charges | 22,000 | Per semester | Hostel residents |
| Institute caution deposit | 10,000 | One time, refundable | All students, at admission |
| Hostel caution deposit | 5,000 | One time, refundable | Hostel residents, at first allotment |
| Continuation fee (break of study) | 5,000 | Per semester of break | Students on an approved break |
| Supplementary examination fee | 800 | Per course, per attempt | As applicable |
| Re-evaluation fee | 1,000 | Per course | As applicable |
| Late registration fee | 500 | Per occurrence | As applicable |

### 1.2 Revision of Fees
The Board of Governors may revise the fees once a year. Any revision shall not exceed ten per cent of the fee for the preceding year and shall be announced before the commencement of the odd semester.

## 2. Payment Deadlines

### 2.1 Semester Fee Deadlines
Fees must be paid by the due date shown below. Payments after the due date attract the late fee described in Section 3. No payment is accepted after the last date.

| Payment | Due date | Last date (with late fee) |
|---|---|---|
| Odd semester tuition, examination and development fee | 15 July 2026 | 31 July 2026 |
| Even semester tuition, examination and development fee | 5 January 2027 | 20 January 2027 |
| Odd semester hostel rent and mess charges | 10 July 2026 | 25 July 2026 |
| Even semester hostel rent and mess charges | 5 January 2027 | 20 January 2027 |
| Supplementary examination fee | Within 7 days of notification | Not applicable |
| Re-evaluation fee | Within 10 working days of the declaration of results | Not applicable |

### 2.2 Instalments
The tuition fee for a semester may be paid in two equal instalments on a written request to the Finance Office made before the due date. The first instalment is payable by the due date and the second instalment within forty-five days of the due date. The late fee under Section 3 applies to each instalment separately.

## 3. Late Fee

### 3.1 Rate of Late Fee
A late fee of Rs. 100 per day is charged on any fee paid after the due date, up to and including the last date shown in Section 2.1. The late fee is capped at Rs. 1,500 for any single payment.

### 3.2 Payment after the Last Date
Fees are not accepted after the last date. A student who has not paid the fees by the last date is treated as not registered for the semester, unless the Registrar, on a written request made within five working days after the last date, permits payment on the ground of a documented delay in the disbursement of an education loan or a scholarship. Where the Registrar permits such a payment, the late fee is charged up to the date of payment.

### 3.3 Waiver of Late Fee
The late fee is not waived except by the Registrar, on a written request explaining the reasons for the delay submitted along with the payment.

## 4. Refund on Withdrawal of Admission

### 4.1 Refund of Tuition Fee
A student who withdraws from the programme shall be refunded the tuition fee paid for the semester as follows:

| Date of withdrawal request | Tuition fee refunded |
|---|---|
| Before the commencement of classes | 100 per cent |
| Within 15 days of the commencement of classes | 80 per cent |
| Between 16 and 30 days of the commencement of classes | 50 per cent |
| After 30 days of the commencement of classes | Nil |

### 4.2 Other Components
The examination fee and the development and laboratory fee are not refundable after the commencement of classes. The Institute caution deposit and the hostel caution deposit are refundable in full in all cases, after the adjustment of any dues. Hostel rent and mess charges are refunded on a pro-rata basis for the unexpired part of the semester where the room is vacated within thirty days of the commencement of classes, and are not refunded thereafter.

### 4.3 Procedure
A request for withdrawal must be submitted in writing to the Registrar on the withdrawal form available on the student portal. The date of receipt of the form in the Registrar's office is treated as the date of withdrawal. Refunds are processed within thirty working days of the receipt of the form and are credited to the bank account from which the fee was paid.

## 5. Mode of Payment

### 5.1 Online Payment Only
All fees are payable only through the online payment portal of the Institute using net banking, UPI, or debit or credit cards. Cash, cheques and demand drafts are not accepted. A payment is treated as made on the date on which it is credited to the Institute's account.

### 5.2 Receipts
An electronic receipt is generated on the portal for every payment. Students must retain the receipt and produce it when required by the Finance Office, the Hostel Office or the Controller of Examinations.
